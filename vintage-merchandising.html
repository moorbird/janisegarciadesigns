Visual Merchandising

Transforming a local costume shop's basement into a curated vintage vault, and designing the window displays that bring shoppers inside

In my free time, I partnered with an independent costume shop in my city to design and set up their new Vintage Vault and refresh their window displays..

The Vault Before & After Window Displays

THE VAULT

The Vintage Vault is home to the shop's retired rental costumes, pieces that have appeared in New York operas, films and stage productions across the country, with origins spanning the Civil War era to today. With resale on the rise, the shop saw a chance to give these costumes a second life as everyday statement pieces or standout looks for Halloween and parties. Our goal was a shopping experience that felt memorable, fun and easy to navigate.

The Challenge

The basement was a blank slate. It needed a layout that made sense to shoppers browsing for the first time and to staff restocking returns.

My Role

I planned the floor layout, then sorted, merchandised, and styled the costumes across the space.

The Approach

I grouped pieces by era, then by category and color. Statement pieces went in the best-lit areas, and the most-tried-on items went closest to the dressing rooms and mirrors.
