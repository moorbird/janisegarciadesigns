ABOUT ME

I'm a Chicago-based UI/UX designer who got my start in a very different world: costume design for film and television. After graduating from UCLA's School of Theater, Film and Television, I spent several years in costume departments, learning how every stitch and color choice helps tell a story. But I wanted my work to do more than entertain. I wanted it to help people.

"Like costume design, UX is about telling a story, except this time the story is helping someone get where they need to go."

Today I design websites and interfaces that turn complex problems into simple, human experiences, drawing on a mix of UX, branding, graphic design, front-end code, and illustration.
